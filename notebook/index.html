The layer GPT and Llama stack · growing one token at a time

One decoder layer, generating

prompt once → then one token at a time, attending to the KV cache

The causal mask — a token cannot look at the future — and the KV cache it makes possible: the prompt's triangle of attention, then one new row per generated token, with the cache on or off. Then one decode step of a real model on a real chip: memory-bound, the cache growing with the context, what batching and grouped-query attention change, and prefill against decode.
